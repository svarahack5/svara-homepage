Everyday care tips

Small habits that make daily life calmer for people with dementia and the people caring for them.

🕰️

Keep a routine

Meals, walks and bedtime at the same time each day help reduce confusion.

🗣️

Speak simply

Use short, calm sentences and give one instruction at a time.

🎵

Music and photos

Familiar songs and old photos can bring comfort and spark good memories.

💜

Look after yourself

Caregivers need rest too. Ask for help, or talk to others in the forum.
